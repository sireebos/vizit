--- FAQ SECTION ---
FAQ Subtitle: frequently asked questions
FAQ Title: Hotel Policies

FAQ 1 Question: What is your cancellation policy?
FAQ 1 Answer: Cancellations made 48 hours or more before check-in receive a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours of check-in are charged one night's stay. No-shows are charged the full booking amount. For group bookings of 5 rooms or more, cancellation must be made 7 days in advance for a full refund. Peak season and holiday bookings may have different cancellation terms which will be communicated at the time of booking.

FAQ 2 Question: What are the check-in and check-out times?
FAQ 2 Answer: Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out time is 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in and late check-out may be arranged subject to availability and may incur additional charges. For early arrivals, we recommend booking the previous night to guarantee room availability. Express check-out is available for guests with valid credit cards on file.

FAQ 3 Question: What is your refund policy?
FAQ 3 Answer: Refunds for cancellations are processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method. Prepaid bookings cancelled within policy receive full refunds minus any applicable booking fees. Refunds for special promotions or discounted rates may have different terms. Incidental charges and minibar expenses are non-refundable and settled at check-out.

FAQ 4 Question: Do I need to print my booking confirmation?
FAQ 4 Answer: No, you don't need to print your confirmation. Simply present your booking confirmation number and a valid government-issued ID at check-in. You can show the confirmation email on your mobile device. For corporate bookings, the authorized booker's details should match the check-in documents.

FAQ 5 Question: How do I join your loyalty program?
FAQ 5 Answer: Our Kuwait Cabin Rewards program is free to join. Simply request enrollment at check-in or visit our website to sign up. Members earn points on room stays, dining, and spa services that can be redeemed for free nights, upgrades, and exclusive experiences. Benefits include member-only rates, priority check-in, late check-out privileges, and special birthday rewards.
